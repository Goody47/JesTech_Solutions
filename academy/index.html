FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is this program for? +
Career changers, recent grads, freelancers, and anyone looking to break into business operations or AI-enabled roles. No prior business experience required—just commitment to put in 8–10 hours per week for 12 weeks.
How much time per week is needed? +
Expect 8–10 hours per week: 2 hours for live cohort calls (Thursdays 7–8:30 PM UK), 4–6 hours for lessons and assignments, plus optional office hours and community time. You control the exact schedule.
What if I've never worked in business? +
No problem. The program starts from fundamentals. Many of our students are career changers or recent graduates with no business background. We teach you the concepts, tools, and frameworks step-by-step.
Do you guarantee a job? +
No one can ethically guarantee a job, but we provide everything you need to succeed: a portfolio, curated job opportunities, application coaching, interview prep, and mentoring. Most graduates land roles within 30–60 days of completing the program.
When does the next cohort start? +
Cohort #1 starts 15th June 2026. Subsequent cohorts roughly every 8 weeks. Early applications get priority—we cap each cohort at 15 students.
How many portfolio pieces do I actually build? +
15+. Nine are real client-style projects built around one running UK small-business case study — a business audit, project charter, business analysis, automation proposal, dashboard, executive presentation, and capstone. The other six are career assets you'll use directly in your job search: optimised CV, LinkedIn profile, professional bio, AI prompt library, job tracker, and STAR story library. By Week 12, all 15 live on a public portfolio website you build in Week 2.
What tools will I need? +
A laptop with internet, plus accounts on free or freemium tools (ChatGPT, Google Docs, Calendly). We'll guide you through anything else as needed.
Is there a refund policy? +
Yes. Full refund within 7 days of purchase, no questions. After that, no refunds—but we're committed to your success. If you're struggling, we'll work with you, including pausing or rejoining a later cohort.
What's the cohort size? +
We cap cohorts at 15 students. Small enough that you'll get personal attention and your peers will know your name. Large enough to have a vibrant community.
