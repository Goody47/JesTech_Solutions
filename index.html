The problem

Running a busy business shouldn't mean running yourself into the ground.

Most small businesses lose revenue not because demand is low — but because the same bottlenecks repeat every single day. We've heard them all.

01 · Missed

Calls during the rush.

Every unanswered call is a paying customer choosing a competitor before you've even seen the missed-call notification.

02 · Repeated

The same answers, daily.

"What are your hours?" "Do you do X?" Your team types the same reply 30 times a day instead of doing real work.

03 · Lost

Bookings after hours.

Enquiries roll in at 11pm. By morning, they've gone cold or booked elsewhere. After-hours is when most decisions get made.

04 · Buried

Drowning in admin.

Follow-ups, reminders, reviews, invoices, posting on socials — by the time it's done, the day is gone.
