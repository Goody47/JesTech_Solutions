For parents & teachers 👋

Galaxy Defender is a self-paced Python course designed for kids ages 7–16. No prior coding experience required — every concept is taught inside the game your child wants to build.

💡 About ages 7–9: the youngest readers will get the most out of the course when an adult sits with them for the first 2–3 lessons (to read the explanations aloud and help with typing). From Lesson 3 onwards most confident readers can self-pace with light check-ins.

Who this course is perfect for

  • Parents who want a screen-time activity they're proud of — something to do with your kid on a Saturday morning instead of watching them scroll.
  • Homeschool families looking for a complete, structured computing project for 6–8 weeks of the term.
  • Classroom teachers running an after-school coding club or a weekly tech lesson — fully self-contained, printable handouts included.
  • Nannies & au pairs who want an enriching activity that holds attention without screens going passive.
  • Holiday clubs & summer camps — works as a 5-day intensive or spread across the holiday.
  • Solo kids with a laptop and curiosity. Many 10–14-year-olds binge the whole course in a couple of weekends.

Practical details

  • Time commitment: 1–2 hours per week, 11 lessons total. Whole course completable in 6–8 weeks; binge-able in a few weekends.
  • No installs: everything runs in the browser via Trinket. Works on any laptop (Windows, Mac, Chromebook, Linux).
  • Printable: every lesson + Code Library prints cleanly so teachers can hand them out (use the Teacher Guide below).
  • Safe environment: no chat, no in-game purchases, no accounts collecting your child's data. Just a coding editor and a password gate.
  • Real skills: variables, loops, lists, functions, game loops, debugging — the same building blocks behind every 2D game and most web/mobile apps.
