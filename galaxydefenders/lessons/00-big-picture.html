🚀 Before we start — what to expect

Two quick questions. Click each to open the answer.

Q1. Will I be writing REAL code?

Yes — real Python, real pygame. By the end you'll have built an arcade game you can share with your friends. Every line of code in this course actually runs.

Q2. What if I get stuck?

Every lesson ships with a working example, an in-browser editor, and a list of tiny challenges. Errors are clues, not punishments — and you can always email nextgencoders@jestechsolutions.co.uk for help.

Every game is a rocket with 4 parts

It doesn't matter if your game is about spaceships, baking cakes, or chasing eggs — every Python game has the same 4 big parts. Once you know the 4 parts, you can build anything. We'll build a space shooter called Galaxy Defender.
